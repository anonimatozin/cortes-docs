Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the cortes application ("cortes", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our application together with the TikTok for Developers platform.

1. Information we collect

TikTok account information (Login Kit): when you sign in with TikTok, we receive your TikTok open_id, display name, avatar, and the account scopes you approved (e.g. user.info.basic). We use this only to identify your account and connect the app to it.

Video content (Content Posting API): when you ask cortes to publish a clip, the video file you selected is transmitted to TikTok through TikTok's Content Posting API (video.upload scope) so it can be posted to your own TikTok account.

Local data: the app stores on your own device: processed video files, configuration, logs, and access/refresh tokens. This data never leaves your computer except when you explicitly publish a video to TikTok.

2. How we use the information

  • To authenticate you with TikTok and keep your session active (token refresh).
  • To upload and publish the clips you choose, to your TikTok account only.
  • To improve the app's reliability (local logs).

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not have servers: all processing happens on your own computer. The only external services contacted are TikTok (and the video platforms you choose to download from).

4. Data storage and retention

Tokens and media are stored locally on your device until you delete them. TikTok token data is handled under TikTok's own policies. You can revoke cortes' access at any time from your TikTok settings → Connected apps, or by deleting the tokens folder of the app.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any data associated with the app. Since data is stored locally, deleting the app folder removes it completely.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). TikTok login requires meeting TikTok's minimum age requirements.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will reflect the latest revision.
